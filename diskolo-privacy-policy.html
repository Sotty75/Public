Diskolo - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Diskolo (previously named Sotty Music) is an Android music and podcast player developed by Paolo Sotgiu, using the name Paolo Sotgiu Produzioni.

Your library and the developer

The app has no user accounts, advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs. It stores your library index, playlists, favourites, metadata corrections, selected covers, podcast subscriptions, listening positions and preferences on your device. It does not automatically send these records to a developer-operated server.

Online searches, podcasts, torrent transfers and the browser library manager send information off the device as described below. If you contact the developer for support, the developer receives the information you choose to include in your message.

Online searches and album covers

When you request an album cover, Diskolo sends the album and artist names, or your search words, to Deezer and to MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive, and downloads the selected image. Automatic cover lookup is off by default. If you enable it in Settings, album and artist names are sent after scans for albums missing artwork. Turn it off to stop future automatic lookups. Embedded covers and images you supply can also be used.

When you request corrected song titles, album and artist names or your search words are sent to Deezer, Apple iTunes Search and MusicBrainz. The app does not use iTunes to fetch album artwork.

Podcasts

Searching or browsing podcasts sends search words, category or catalogue identifiers, and the selected country to Podcast Index and/or Apple's podcast directory and charts services. If you have not selected a country, the app uses the device's region setting; it does not request GPS location.

Browsing, following, refreshing, playing or downloading a podcast contacts its publisher's feed, artwork and episode hosts. Requests reveal which resources are requested. Followed podcasts can be refreshed while the app runs, and playback or downloads can continue in the background.

Internet services receive your IP address and normal network request information, such as the app's user-agent. Their logging, retention and privacy practices are governed by their own policies: Deezer, Apple, MetaBrainz (MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive, with images served by the Internet Archive), Podcast Index, and the relevant podcast publisher or host. Diskolo does not send your contacts, advertising identifier or precise device location to these services.

Torrent transfers

Adding a magnet link or torrent file connects the app to the BitTorrent network, including trackers, DHT nodes and peers. They can receive your IP address, port, torrent identifier and protocol information. Peers exchange pieces of the selected torrent: downloading also uploads pieces to other people.

Continued sharing after completion is off by default. If you enable Keep sharing after download, completed torrents can continue uploading while the app runs. Pause or remove a transfer to stop it. Transfer only material you have permission to download and redistribute. Other participants may retain information they receive; uninstalling the app cannot erase their records or copies.

Wi-Fi transfer and browser library manager

Starting Wi-Fi transfer opens a server on your device's local Wi-Fi or Ethernet interface. A browser that can reach it must provide the code shown on the phone to access library data. Access depends on your network configuration; do not expose the service to the internet or use an untrusted shared network.

The browser can upload music and images, receive library metadata and artwork, stream audio, download a ZIP backup of the music library, edit library entries and request deletion of music files. These transfers take place between the phone and the connected browser, without a developer-operated relay. Online lookups made through the browser still contact the catalogue services described above.

The connection uses HTTP and is not encrypted. The code controls access but does not encrypt traffic. Someone able to observe network traffic could read the code and transferred data. Stop the server when finished; it also stops after its inactivity period or a detected network change.

The browser stores the connection code and interface preferences in local storage. Clear that site's browser data to remove them. Copies saved or downloaded to the computer remain there until you delete them.

Security and permissions

Catalogue requests use HTTPS. Some publisher feeds and media URLs support only HTTP, and BitTorrent traffic is not guaranteed to be encrypted. Diskolo therefore does not promise encryption for every transfer.

  • Music and audio, or storage access on older Android versions: scan and play your music.
  • Notifications and foreground services: playback controls and ongoing transfers.
  • Internet and network state: online features, local transfer and Wi-Fi-only download settings.
  • Wake lock and vibration: keep active playback/transfers running and provide haptic feedback.

Deleting music removes the selected files. Android confirmation is requested where required by Android; files added by Diskolo may be deleted directly. Renaming or regrouping entries within the app does not rewrite the original audio tags.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local library records, settings and cached artwork remain until you remove them through the available app controls, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes app-private data; music imported into shared Music storage and backups saved elsewhere can remain and must be deleted separately.

Turning off automatic cover lookup, stopping transfers, removing podcast subscriptions and avoiding online searches limits future network requests. You can revoke music access in Android Settings. Clearing local data does not delete logs held by third-party services; contact those services about their records and rights requests.

Support correspondence is used to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Contact the developer to request deletion of that correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations. Do not include private music, credentials or other sensitive information unless necessary for your request.

Children

Diskolo is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not designed for children. Podcast content comes from independent publishers and may contain mature material.

Contact and policy changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact Paolo Sotgiu / Paolo Sotgiu Produzioni using the developer contact email displayed on Diskolo's Google Play listing. The developer can address data held by the developer; independent providers handle requests concerning their own records.

Updates to this policy will carry a revised date. The policy is also available offline in Settings > Privacy policy.
